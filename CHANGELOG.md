# Changelog

## 0.7.1 (beta)
Bug fixes: joining over Steam on big houses, shared budgets in jobs that have their own, doors used at the same moment, a restarted host sending its house.

* **Fixed: joining over Steam could loop forever on big houses** (GitHub issue #2): the host's large messages (a house's lawn can be about 2 MB) filled Steam's send buffer, the send failed and the connection was closed, so the player kept reconnecting. Messages now wait in a queue and go out as Steam has room, and the send buffer is raised to 8 MB.
* **Fixed: spending in a job with its own budget was not shared** (farm, DineOut and HGTV jobs): the game spends from the order's budget there, but the mod only shared the profile balance, so each player had their own budget. The budget is now shared like the balance (spending and earning on either side, and a player who joins or rejoins gets the host's budget); the profile balance stays separate. Checked on an HGTV job: host -1000, client -500, client +250, rejoin: same budget on both sides throughout; no budget messages when moving to a house without one.
* **Fixed: a host that restarted could not send its house**: the house snapshot failed to serialize once the floor changes had been loaded from the save ("Could not convert string 'GridCoords: ...' to dictionary key type"); the floor changes are now sent as a list.
* **Fixed: a door used by two players at the same moment could end up open on one side and closed on the other**: a door use now carries the state the door is heading to, and a use that arrives while the door is still moving is retried. Checked: three rounds of both players using the same door at once, same state on both sides.
* Pet AI resume: the game's unsubscribe step is found more reliably.

## 0.7.0 (beta)
The mod is now a beta: it covers the base game and the DLC areas and has been tested on two and three players, on one PC and over a real network.

* **Fixed: eggs could be deleted for good**: eggs, like animals, count as items in the game; when a player's game removed a duplicate egg, the item sync reported it as gone and the host deleted its real egg and recorded it as destroyed in the house save. Eggs and animals never travel as items now (the coop and pet sync share them).
* **Fixed: an item could be deleted on the other side right after a house load**: when a house is left, its runtime items are cleaned up a moment later and looked like sales; the others then deleted the item with the same id, which could be an item on the same spot in the new house. Items that vanish in the first seconds after a house load are not sent any more.
* **Less network traffic for animals**: an animal that has not changed is sent once a second instead of ten times (measured: about 1.5-2 KB/s for a farm's 7 animals, 6-8 KB/s with 27).
* Checked: small animals in a real terrarium (hamster: followed and taken over), switching for nine animal types and the hamster, a third player joining while another player runs animals, and a player on a second PC over the real network (house, items and events received, no errors).
* **Fixed: a farm's animals could disappear for good**: a joining player's game took the house's own animals (the farm's chickens and cows) for leftover pets from its own profile and removed them; its item sync then reported them as gone, and the host deleted its animals and recorded them as destroyed in the house save, so they were missing on every later visit. Only pets from the player's own profile are removed now, and animals never travel as items (the pet sync shares them). Checked with the farm through a whole day, a player leaving and joining again, house switches, reloads and a save: all 7 animals on both sides throughout.
* **Fixed: a house could stop loading after a player rejoined**: resuming a pet's AI could sign it up a second time for the game's scene-change event, and the sign-up left behind after the pet was removed stopped the next house load. Resuming is now safe to repeat.
* **Every animal follows the same rules**: all 21 animal types (dog, cat, bunny, the eight small animals in cages and tanks: hamster, guinea pig, rat, parrot, snake, spider, turtle, iguana and fish, and the farm's cow, chicken, rooster, horse, pig, sheep, alpaca and goat) are run by one game and followed by the others. The small animals walk on their own cage behaviour instead of the pet AI; it is paused and resumed the same way. Checked with one of each type: on both sides, exactly one game runs each animal with its AI set up, the other follows it.
* **Fixed: farm animals drifted on the other player's screen**: the game switched the walking back on for the animals that come with a farm while they followed the host; it is switched off again while an animal follows another game.
* **Pet movement and animation are shared**: each pet is run by one player's game (the host, or the player standing next to it) and the others follow it ten times a second: same spot, same heading, same animation state. Checked with 11 pets: same positions and animation states on both sides; walking, sitting and carrying were also checked by hand.
* **Any player can pick up, pet and feed a pet**: the player who walks up to a pet takes it over (within about 3.5 m; it goes back to the host when they walk more than 6 m away, and the host keeps a pet its own player stands next to), so the interaction runs with the pet's real AI on that player's game. Checked by hand with an adopted pug, switching between host and client several times.
* **Fixed: pets looked different on the other side**: a pet without a save id was matched by its number alone, so the other player's own pet number 1 could stand in for the host's; the type, breed and name must match now.
* **Fixed: pets with no animation after a house load**: a pet that arrived while the house was still loading was made before its walking area existed and was left half made; it now waits until the house has loaded and a failed attempt is cleaned up and tried again.
* **Fixed: a pet whose AI had been paused could never be interacted with again**: switching the game's pet AI off clears its setup, and switching it back on did not redo it; resuming now runs the game's own setup again and clears the interaction state the pet's controllers had kept from before. (This also applied to hens leaving a nest.)
* **Chicken coop nests are shared**: the host's game decides which hen sits on which nest; the other players' own hen AI can no longer take or leave a nest, and a hen the host has on a nest sits in that nest at the same height on every side (the game kept pulling it to the ground under the nest). Checked: same three hens on the same three nests on both sides.
* **Eggs sync both ways**: an egg picked up by one player disappears for the others (checked on both sides: 3, 2, 1 eggs). A nest that held an egg with nothing on it after loading (so nobody could pick it up and no hen laid there again) gets its egg back, and a nest keeps at most one egg.
* **Hoeing and sowing look the same on both sides**: the grass under a newly hoed tile is removed for the others too; sowing a tile the other player sees is done in place (it used to rebuild the tile, which grew the grass back and broke the row); rebuilt tiles are entered in the game's soil grid and joined to their neighbours again, so rows connect instead of showing single plots with stone edges. A crop the other side had no seed data for is rebuilt from its record. Checked by hand with real hoe and Sower clicks.
* **Pet bowl reactions are shared**: eating, drinking and looking at the bowl are now hooked on every pet type (they are declared abstract in the base controller, which is why hooking them failed before).
* **Two leaf blowers at once** work: each player's copy appears on the other side and is removed when blowing stops (also checked with three copies at once).
* **DineOut tableware presets are shared**: the preset library (it lives in each player's progress) is now one shared list: a preset saved or removed by one player appears or disappears for the others. Checked with twelve adds and deletes in both directions, all identical afterwards. (The preset list travels as a whole; if two players change it in the same second the later change wins.)
* **Pet reactions are shared**: being petted and taking a snack now play on the other players' copy of the pet (the game's own reaction calls are forwarded).
* **Leaf blower**: while a player blows, the position of their force field is sent and the others get a copy of it. The copy's fields are now added to the leaf piles' own force list (the piles only react to fields on that list, which is why a copy that merely stood in the scene did nothing), and a player who has never used the blower gets the copy as their blower system. Checked with real blowing on two game windows: 76 force-field updates sent and received, the copy removed when blowing stopped, and the leaves were seen moving in real time on both sides.
* **A player who joins later gets the current state of tables, bedding, coops, bowls, leaf piles and renovated furniture**: those states used to be sent only when they changed, so a joiner kept the defaults (a leaf pile showed 1408 leaves for the joiner and 460 for the host). The host now sends them when a joiner's house is ready.
* **Fixed: a preset or other progress change made here could be lost** when a progress message from the other player arrived in the same second (applying it re-baselined everything, including the change that had not been sent yet).
* **Fixed: one failing tracker no longer stops the others** (the trackers run one after another every frame; an exception in an early one used to skip the progress sync for that frame). A failing tracker is now logged once.
* **Hens laying eggs by themselves**: the hens never entered the coop because the coop door was shut (the door has to be opened first; hens then take the nests within seconds, and with the game sped up all three nests held an egg). Both players' hens lay in their own game, so an egg arriving from the other player could stack a second egg on a nest: the coop now keeps one egg per nest (checked: host and client both show 3 eggs on 3 nests).

## 0.6.0
* **Farm weather is shared**: the next weather (clear, clouds, rain) was rolled with `Random` on every player's own game, so rain and sun differed between players after the first change. The host's pick is sent and clients use it (a pick the client rolled first is replaced as soon as the host's arrives). Checked over four forced weather changes: the queued weather was the same on both players every time. The moment a change starts can still differ by the time the second player's house finished loading.
* **Pets and farm animals are shared**: a pet spawned on the host (adopted, loaded, bought) did not exist for the other player. The host now sends each pet as the game's own save record when it appears (the client builds it with the game's own pet loader), tells when it is gone and sends positions twice a second; a pet that drifted more than 0.75 m is put back (the local AI keeps animating it). Pets are told apart by their save guid (the first pet has id 0, and pets created together shared an id, so some never arrived). Pets from the joiner's own profile that the game loads into the host's house are removed after the host's list has arrived. Checked with a dog, cat and chicken (id 0) spawned together: all matched on both players. Animations (eating, being petted) are not shared, only positions and stats.
* **More of the recording is shared**: rolled turf, magnets kept unpainted and prefab swaps (garden / pets / repair jobs) now go to the other player.
* **Pet stats are shared both ways**: when hunger, thirst, happiness or energy of a pet jumps on one player (fed, watered, petted), the new values go to the other. Checked by setting stats on each side (the feeding/petting animation itself is not shared).
* **DineOut tablecloths are shared**: the cloth, its texture, ruffle and length now reach the other player (changes to tables are re-checked every second and sent like any item change). Also for Luxury renovated furniture (the renovated state of all parts arrived; the exact paint colour did not match in my test, which used generated data rather than the real renovation mini-game) and the same check covers pet bowls/containers, chicken coops, animal bedding and leaf piles (not tested).
* **Farm soil and crops are shared in both directions**: hoed tiles, sowing, growth and harvesting reach the other player (the game only writes soil to the save when it saves). Checked in the starter garden and on a farm plot, driving the tools' own routines (the hoe's placement and the seed-dictionary sowing step the Sower uses; literal mouse clicks never placed soil in my tests): a hoed tile, sown corn, both players' corn at the same stage (6/6) after the same game time, harvesting on the host (crop gone for both) and a client-side sowing appearing on the host. Late joiners get the current tiles; duplicate tiles on one cell are cleaned up (the tile with the crop always wins) and a seeded tile that never grew its crop is grown with the game's own sowing step. A stray crop at the world origin seen after sowing comes from the spawned seed item, not from the mod.
* **UFO event and gravity machinery**: using the portal panel (shows the circle puzzle), turning puzzle circles, finishing the puzzle, the gravity lever and the gravity lift are sent to the other player. Checked: panel, circles, completion, the lever (both players' view flipped upright to inverted and back, with identical gravity-manager state) and the lift. The Molly rescue sequence itself was not watched.
* **Luxury furniture renovation**: finishing every part through the real mini-game routine (start, finish all parts, return from the garage) gave the other player the same renovated parts and the same paint colours.
* **The farm drone is shown to the other player**: a flying drone's position is sent five times a second and a drone is shown at that spot for the other player. Checked: it appeared, followed a 3 m move and disappeared when the flight ended.
* **Pet bowls, animal bedding, chicken coops and leaf piles** are covered by the same state check: a bowl filled to 4 and emptied once (3), the bedding use count, a coop egg and a leaf pile burned from 1408 to 460 leaves all reached the other player with identical values. (An extra loader step that doubled coop eggs was removed: the game's own state load already restores all of these.) The egg was made through the game's load path, not laid by a hen, and the leaf blower itself was not tested.

* **Missing DLC is handled instead of failing silently**: joining players now report which DLCs they own and the host reports its own. A player who lacks a DLC the host has sees a plain note, and the host sees which player lacks what. When the host loads a house that needs a DLC a player does not own, that player is told ("needs the Farm DLC") instead of being sent a house they cannot build, and they follow the host again as soon as it moves to a house they can load. Setting `Network / RequireSameDlc` (default off) refuses players who lack any DLC the host owns. Older mod versions do not report DLCs and get no checks. Checked on two machines: a second PC that really owns only Cyberpunk and Apocalypse joined over the network; the host listed what it lacks, refused to send it a Farm house (both players saw the message in their top-left banner), and the PC followed the host again into a Cyberpunk house it owns. Also checked with a third player present (the other PC joined next to a local client and received the host's item changes). `Debug / PretendMissingDlc` fakes a missing DLC for single-PC testing.
* Not covered: pet feeding/petting animations, the leaf blower's own blowing, a hen laying an egg by itself, DineOut tableware presets (a per-player library, not house state), and literal mouse clicks on the hoe and Sower.

## 0.5.3
* **Laying a lot of floor tiles no longer freezes the other player's game**: applying a batch of tile changes compared every new record against every existing one by re-serialising the whole list each time, and the sender re-serialised every record twice a second. In a test, 1000 tile changes at once froze the other game for 3-4 s; now the worst frame was 19 ms. Slow applies are logged with a per-phase timing.
* **Grass cut by a mower, scythe or flamethrower is shared**: the lawn mask is sent to the other players when somebody stops cutting, and to a player who joins later. Verified: identical mask on both players after a cut.
* **An outdated-version notice** (text only, no links) is shown when a newer release exists (setting `CheckForUpdates`).
* **Buying and placing objects and doors counts toward the job for the other player**, and door/window installations raise the game's installation event on the other player (checked in the Christmas Carol Past job: the door purchase progress synced).
* **Light bulbs**: a bulb broken or repaired by one player is applied to the other and the repair quests recount on it (checked on a job house: identical bulb states on both players).
* **The bunker entrance (and other doors/hatches the game does not give an id)** is addressed by its scene path, so it opens for both players (checked: all 30 doors, windows and hatches of the bunker job matched after toggling them).
* **Shower and other mount progress** is retried when the device is not there yet, so a "mounted" message is no longer lost (checked: a shower mounted by the host was mounted for the client).
* **Job items sold by any path are noticed** (every scene item is watched, not only the ones that were moved) and stale office doors from an earlier house are cleared when a house changes.
* **A failing quest can no longer hang a house load**: load-time event raisers are guarded one subscriber at a time.

## 0.5.2
* **A client's item from a Workshop addon the host does not have is refused**: the host cannot build it (and the game would have paid the host the item's price for a "missing" item), so it drops the event, does not pass it on to the other players, and tells the client, which sells the item back with the game's own sale (the refund the game gives for a missing item). Verified with a client holding an addon the host lacks.
* **A door/window that is open on the host is open for a client that joins**: a "use" is a toggle and the open state is not saved with the house, so a joining client had every door closed and the host closing its open door opened it on the client. The host now counts the uses of every two-state door/window/lid since the house was loaded and replays the odd ones to a client when it reports its house is loaded (verified: the host opened a window and the front door before the client joined, the client received both).
* **After leaving a session the client goes back to the main menu** (instead of standing in the host's house): it picks its own save from there. Verified on the second PC.
* **Steam Workshop items**: when the host's house contains Workshop items you are not subscribed to, the client subscribes to them for the length of the session, waits for Steam to download them, and then loads the house; items the host places later are placed once their download finishes. When you disconnect the mod unsubscribes again (only from what it subscribed to itself; a list file covers a crash). A client no longer gets the sale price of a missing Workshop item paid out (the shared money would have passed it on to the host). Config `Workshop / TemporaryDownloads` (default on) turns the downloads off. Tested with a second PC on a different Steam account that had none of the addons: all 10 items were subscribed, downloaded and built before the house loaded, items placed later were downloaded and placed, and everything it subscribed to was unsubscribed again when the client disconnected. Then, with all 10 addons placed at once: moving an item syncs host to client and client to host, selling one syncs both ways (the seller gets the money, the other player's item disappears), and the items show up for the client (Kuromi, the cats, Pou and the Minecraft bed were seen on both screens). Not checked: using the items, and the look of the other five addons (4K TV, mirror, Pringles, mug, bookshelf).
* **House load retry is a setting** (`Network / LoadRetrySeconds`, default 40 s; it was 20 s): a slower PC needs longer than 20 s for a healthy load and was made to fetch the house a second time. A retry is also held back while the game is visibly busy (main thread blocked).

## 0.5.1
* **Moving the office now reaches the client**: when the host makes a house its office and puts the office PC down, the clients load the house again with the office already switched, so it no longer looks like a house for sale to them (no for-sale appraisers) and no second office PC ends up in their hands.
* **Doors that come with a house work after the host reloads it** (main menu and back, or a house change): they get an id both players compute from the door's prefab and place instead of a host-made one the client could not find, ids are only given once the house has settled, and an unknown position id is looked up by position. This also fixes the front door not opening for the client after a reload.
* **Leftover doors/windows from an earlier load are removed sooner** (2 s after a load, repeated every 4 s), so the short-lived double is much less visible.

## 0.5.0
* **The office is taken over before the host's house loads** (no second office PC in the client's hands, no "for sale" buyers outside). **The office the host claims is shared**: the host sends which house is its office (and when it was created) when you join and whenever it changes; the client swaps it in, so the host's office no longer looks like a house on sale, and puts its own office back when the session ends.
* **Doors/windows left over from an earlier load are removed**: loading a house a second time in one run (host to the main menu and back, or a house change) left the first load's doors/windows standing next to the new ones. The check now also removes those leftovers (any door/window item of an installed kind that no record points to and that stands at the same spot as a recorded one); it runs a few times in the first minute after a load. Verified on the host with a reloaded house.
* **Doubled doors and windows already in a save are removed**: after a house has loaded, every peer checks its own house for two doors or windows of the same kind at the same spot and removes the extra one locally (nothing is sent, so a peer that does not have the double is not affected).
* **A quest that fails while a house loads no longer blocks the load** (the client got stuck on "Loading host's house"); the load retry now fires after 20 s instead of 90 s.
* **Doubled doors/windows after a job and a client seeing office doors inside a job**: when the host changed house (job -> office and back) the mod compared the new house with what it had sent for the old one, so every door/window/record looked "new" (and every old one "gone") and was sent to a client that was still in the old house. The mod now resets what it remembers the moment the host changes house, tells the clients first ("changing"), ignores events about the old house on both sides, and the client reports "ready" once it is in the new house.
* **Saves with duplicate records are cleaned** (when the host saves and when any save is loaded): duplicate door/window installation records, replaced-window records, item records and other doubles are merged, so doors/windows are not built twice when the office is loaded. A save that already has doubled windows is repaired when it is loaded.
* **Re-applying an installation to a door/window that already has one** (frame and plug) no longer builds a second one next to it, and loaded installations always get an id both players share.
* **Job progress now follows paint, tile and panel work done by the other player**: the painting/tiling quests recount on events that only the paint and tile tools raise, so work applied from the other player never updated them. The same events are now raised after a remote wall/floor/ceiling change (found from the quests' own subscriptions). Not verified in a job yet.
* **Snowdrifts (and rubbish heaps) cleared by the other player** now disappear for you and count toward the job.
* **Replaced items (stairs, windows...)**: the replacement quests are pointed at the replaced item and the game's "replacement confirmed" event is raised, so the job counts them (verified for stairs replaced by the host).

## About 0.4.7 and 0.4.8
These two versions were internal builds and were never published: they did not feel finished enough to put in public, so the first public version after 0.4.6 is 0.4.9. Everything from 0.4.7 and 0.4.8 is included in 0.4.9 and listed below.

## 0.4.9
* **Doors and windows built during the session now work for the other player**: opening/closing syncs and selling removes them (frame included). The cause was that the game finishes setting up a freshly placed installation a moment after it is applied, so the mod could not tell which item was the door; it is now found by prefab and position. A sold door/window is also announced when its installation record disappears, and the other peer removes the installation together with the item.
* **Doors and windows audit**: every door/window/opener class in the game was checked against what the mod syncs.
* **Smart doors now sync** (smart-home doors only react to a different call than normal doors; the mod always replayed the normal one). A use that did not really happen is no longer replicated either.
* **Safe doors, scissor-lift doors, animated openers and doorbells** are replicated now.
* **Items that come with a house** (job furniture, windows/doors made by the replace-window/door quests) get an id that both players agree on, so their doors, windows and cupboards sync and moving them no longer creates duplicates. A brand-new item is sent to the other player before its first use.

## 0.4.8
* **Lamps stay lit**: a lamp that is switched on no longer goes dark when you leave its room (the game only lit lamps in the room you were in). Config `AlwaysLitLamps` (default on) turns this off.
* **Perk popups**: a client no longer levels up by itself, which made the "new perk" popup repeat every time the tool was used. The host still decides perk points; the client just follows.
* **Tiles and paint on newly built walls** now sync (walls built by the other player are registered in the floor's wall list, as the wall tool does).
* **Items that disappear without a sale event** (for example doors installed during the session and then sold) are now noticed and removed for everyone; this covers doors, windows, appliances and lights alike.
* Discord (from 0.4.7): the game's native Discord SDK stays disabled and the plugin shows its own presence.

## 0.4.7
* **Fixes a game crash**: the game's native Discord SDK (`discord_game_sdk.dll`) can panic when Discord is closed or in an odd state (seen as a crash right after a co-op session ended, in `ActivityManager.ClearActivity`). The plugin now always switches that SDK off.
* **Own Discord Rich Presence** written in managed code (Discord's local IPC pipe, background thread, reconnects by itself): it shows House Flipper with the current house, and for co-op "Hosting / Joined a session - N players" with a party size. No player names are sent. Turn it off with `DiscordPresence = false` (`[UI]` in the config); the native SDK stays off either way.

## 0.4.6
* **New player avatars**: a small articulated figure per player (cap and shirt in the player's colour, walking legs, arms that reach for what they hold) instead of a capsule.
* **Held tools are drawn as blocky models** (sledgehammer, nail hammer, axe, chainsaw, shovel, paint roller, brush, trowel, cleaners, vacuum, pressure washer and sprayers, shotgun, price scanner, wall/floor tools) and the tiling/panel tool is a flat block with a thin white tile.
* **Tool animations**: while a player works with a tool (a mouse button held in game) the others see it swing, stroke, scrub, recoil or press; idle tools breathe slightly.
* Network protocol 4: the player state now carries "is using the tool", so **everyone must update to 0.4.6** (older versions refuse to connect with a clear message).

## 0.4.5
* Floor tiles now show up for the other player (the floor's combined mesh is rebuilt after a remote change).
* Light switches and lamps bought while connected work for both players (lamp on/off states are synced directly).
* Buying and placing job items, and selling them, update the other player's job task list the way a local drop/sale does.
* Joining a job house is more robust: a newer house arriving during a load is queued, a load that never finishes is retried after 90 s instead of leaving "Loading host's house...".

## 0.4.4

* Doors and windows: every install stage, selling, and open/close (also doors placed in earlier sessions) sync; the wall inside a new door opens on both sides.

* Perk points and perk levels are host-authoritative.
* Selling an item while another player is working on it no longer leaves them stuck in the zoom camera (same fix for windows finished by someone else).
